# Verify and replace evidence

## Purpose

Get an independent decision on a piece of evidence — accepted or rejected —
and, when evidence needs updating, replace it without losing the original.

## When to use this

- Evidence has been recorded and someone needs to confirm it is acceptable
  for what it supports.
- Evidence was rejected and better evidence is now available.
- Accepted evidence has been renewed or improved (for example a newer
  photo, certificate or report).

This instruction is about **evidence**. To judge whether an **action's
work** resolved the issue, see
[work instruction 08](08-perform-verification.md). The two are separate
decisions.

## Who can do this

- **Managers and admins** submit evidence for verification, withdraw it,
  correct it before a decision, and record replacements.
- **Managers and admins** who are **independent** accept or reject it.
- **Viewers** can see everything but change nothing.

## Before you start

- The evidence must already be recorded
  ([work instruction 07](07-add-and-link-evidence.md)).
- Evidence for a **contractor requirement** is decided in Contractor
  assurance ([work instruction 14](14-manage-contractor-assurance.md)), not
  here.

## Steps

**Submit evidence for verification**

1. Open the evidence (**Assurance → Evidence**, then the reference).
2. Under **Verification**, select **Submit for verification**, then confirm.
   (You can also tick **Submit for verification now** when adding evidence.)
   The evidence is now **Awaiting verification** and appears on the
   **Verification** page.

**Accept or reject it** (someone independent)

3. Go to **Assurance → Verification**. Under **Evidence awaiting
   verification**, the **You can decide** column shows **Yes** or **Not
   independent**.
4. Open the evidence and select **Accept or reject**.
5. Choose **Accept**, or **Reject** with a **Reason**, then **Record
   decision**.

**Correct it before a decision**

6. While evidence is **Unverified** or **Awaiting verification**, select
   **Correct details**, change what is wrong, then **Save correction**. The
   previous details are kept in the history.

**Replace it**

7. Open evidence that is **Accepted** or **Rejected** and select **Record
   replacement**. Enter the new evidence's details, then **Record
   replacement**. The replacement is linked to the same open records.
8. Submit the replacement for verification (or tick **Submit for
   verification now**), and have it decided as in steps 3–5.

## What happens next

- **Accepted** evidence is current. Nothing else changes: no action,
  finding, inspection, audit, incident or contractor requirement is
  verified, closed or completed by it.
- **Rejected** evidence stays in the history, with the reason. Record a
  replacement if better evidence is available. No finding is created.
- When a replacement of accepted evidence is **accepted**, the earlier
  evidence becomes **Superseded** and stays readable, unchanged, under
  **Replacement history**.
- When a replacement is **rejected**, the evidence it was meant to replace
  stays exactly as it was.

## Important rules

- The person who **recorded** or **captured** the evidence cannot accept or
  reject it. Neither can the **owner** of an action it supports, or anyone
  who has **completed that action's work**.
- BrainBase can only check people it knows. It cannot prove independence
  from an external supplier.
- Evidence can be corrected only before a decision. After a decision,
  record a replacement instead.
- Only the **current** evidence in a chain can be replaced: accepted
  evidence, or rejected evidence when nothing newer is current or in
  progress. Superseded evidence cannot be replaced.
- One replacement at a time: while a replacement is waiting, another cannot
  be started for the same evidence.
- Nothing is deleted. Decisions, corrections, links and replacements are all
  kept in the history.
- There is no file upload. The evidence says where the original is held.

## Common issues

| Message or problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Verification must be independent: …" | Ask another manager or admin to decide. |
| "Reason is required." | Enter why the evidence is rejected. |
| "Evidence can only be corrected before it is accepted or rejected. Record a replacement instead." | Use **Record replacement**. |
| "This evidence is not the current evidence in its chain; replace the current evidence instead." | Open the current evidence (see **Replacement history**) and replace that. |
| "Another replacement for this evidence is already in progress." | Have the waiting replacement decided first. |
| "This evidence belongs to a contractor submission…" | Decide it in Contractor assurance. |
| "Someone else changed this evidence since you opened it." | Reload the page and try again. |
| **Accept or reject** not shown | You are not independent, you have viewer access, or the evidence is not awaiting verification. |
